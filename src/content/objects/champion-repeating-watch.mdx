A quarter-repeater strikes the hour and the quarter on demand, for telling the time in the dark. The outer case is glazed and jewelled; the working case inside it is plain gold. The museum catalogues the two cases as one object in two parts, 17.190.1440a, b, which is why a single plate carries both.
