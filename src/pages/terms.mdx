## The catalogue

Entries may be quoted with attribution, and linked to freely. A number, a title and a date are
facts about an object and belong to no one.

## The photographs

Each plate is a studio photograph and is not released for reuse. Reproduction in print or online
needs written permission, which is usually given for editorial and scholarly work. Ask from the
[contact page](/contact) and name the catalogue number.

## The objects

Some objects are in private hands and are catalogued with the owner's consent. A listing here is
not an offer to sell.

## Accuracy

Dates and attributions are the studio's best reading of the evidence and are revised when better
evidence turns up. Corrections are welcome.
