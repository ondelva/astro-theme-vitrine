## What is collected

This site sets no cookies. If analytics are switched on in `src/config.ts`, they are aggregate
only: page views, referrers and country, with nothing that identifies a reader.

## The enquiry form

Messages sent from the [contact page](/contact) are handled by the form service named in
`src/config.ts`, under that service's own privacy policy. Nothing is stored on this site.

## Images

The plates are served from this site. No image is loaded from a third party, so viewing a page
tells no one else that you did.
